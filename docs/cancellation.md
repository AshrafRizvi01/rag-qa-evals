# Cancellation Policy

This policy applies to all travel packages purchased through the Sunshine Travels website.

## Standard Cancellation Terms

| Travel Type   | Cancellation Window           | Fee |
|---------------|--------------------------------|-----|
| Domestic      | 30+ days before travel date    | 10% of total package value |
| International | 90+ days before travel date    | 10% of total package value |
| Domestic / International (within above windows) | Less than the above notice periods | 50% of total package value |

## Special Circumstances

Travel plans marked as **special circumstances** (as indicated within the plan itself) follow the cancellation dates and terms defined by that specific plan, rather than the standard terms above.

## Zero Cancellation Plans

Plans designated as **zero cancellation** may only be cancelled up to **30 days** before the travel date. No cancellations are permitted after this window.
