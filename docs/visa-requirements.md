# Visa Requirements Policy

- Visa requirements vary by destination and are the **traveler's responsibility** unless visa assistance is explicitly included in the plan.
- Sunshine Travels can provide **visa assistance** (documentation guidance, application support) for an additional fee, where offered.
- Required documents typically include a valid passport, photographs, proof of funds, and travel itinerary.
- Visa processing times vary by country; travelers should apply at least **4–6 weeks before** the travel date.
- Visa fees are **non-refundable**, and visa rejection does not entitle the traveler to a package refund beyond standard cancellation terms.
