# Refunds Policy

Sunshine Travels processes refunds for eligible cancellations as follows:

- **Approved refunds** are issued to the original payment method within **7–10 business days**.
- Refund amount is calculated after deducting any applicable **cancellation fee** (see [Cancellation Policy](refunds.md)).
- **Non-refundable** charges (e.g., visa fees, insurance, service fees) are excluded from the refund amount.
- No refunds are issued for **no-shows** or cancellations made after the travel date.
- Refunds for payments made via bank transfer may take an additional **3–5 business days** to reflect.

For refund status queries, contact support with your booking reference number.
