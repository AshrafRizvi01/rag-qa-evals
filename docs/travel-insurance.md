# Travel Insurance Policy

- Travel insurance is **mandatory for all international travel plans** and optional for domestic plans.
- Coverage includes trip cancellation, medical emergencies, lost baggage, and flight delays, subject to the insurer's terms.
- Insurance premiums are charged separately and are **non-refundable** once the policy is issued.
- Claims must be filed directly with the insurance provider, along with supporting documentation (bills, reports, receipts).
- Sunshine Travels acts only as a facilitator and is not liable for claim approvals or payouts.
