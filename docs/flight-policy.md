# Flight Policy

- Flights are booked through partner airlines and are subject to the **airline's own fare, baggage, and change rules**.
- Seat selection and meal preferences must be requested at least **48 hours before** departure.
- Missed flights due to late check-in are **not eligible for rebooking or refund** by Sunshine Travels.
- Flight delays or cancellations by the airline are handled per the airline's policy; Sunshine Travels will assist with rebooking where possible.
- Any fare difference for flight changes or upgrades is payable by the traveler.
