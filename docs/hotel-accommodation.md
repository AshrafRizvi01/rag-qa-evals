# Hotel & Accommodation Policy

- Standard **check-in time is 2:00 PM** and **check-out time is 11:00 AM**, subject to the hotel's own policy.
- Room upgrades are subject to availability and may incur an additional charge payable at the hotel.
- **No-shows** at a hotel without prior cancellation are charged the full cost of that night's stay.
- Room sharing is based on **double occupancy** unless a single or triple occupancy is requested at booking.
- Any damages caused to hotel property by the traveler are payable directly to the hotel.
