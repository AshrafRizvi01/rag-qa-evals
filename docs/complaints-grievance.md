# Complaints & Grievance Policy

- Complaints can be submitted via email, phone, or the website's support form with your booking reference number.
- Sunshine Travels will acknowledge all complaints within **48 hours** of receipt.
- A resolution or detailed response will be provided within **7 business days**.
- Unresolved complaints may be escalated to the **Grievance Officer** for further review.
- Escalated cases are reviewed and closed within **15 business days** of escalation.
