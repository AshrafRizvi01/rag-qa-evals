# Passport Information

Passport details are **mandatory** for all international travel plans booked through Sunshine Travels.

- Passport must be **valid for at least 6 months** beyond the return travel date.
- Full name, passport number, nationality, and expiry date must match the passport exactly.
- Passport details must be submitted at the time of booking, or at least **14 days before** the travel date.
- Bookings with incomplete or incorrect passport details may be **delayed or cancelled** without refund.
- Visa requirements, if applicable, are the traveler's responsibility unless otherwise stated in the plan.
