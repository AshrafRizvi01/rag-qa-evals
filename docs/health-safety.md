# Health & Safety Policy

- Travelers must disclose any pre-existing medical conditions at the time of booking.
- Destination-specific **vaccination requirements** must be met before travel; proof may be requested.
- A signed **medical fitness declaration** is required for high-altitude, adventure, or extreme activity packages.
- Sunshine Travels reserves the right to deny boarding to travelers who do not meet health or safety requirements, without refund.
- Travelers are advised to check current **health advisories** for their destination before departure.
