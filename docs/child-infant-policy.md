# Child & Infant Policy

- **Infants** (under 2 years) travel at **10% of the adult package price** and are not guaranteed a separate seat.
- **Children** (2–11 years) are charged **50% of the adult package price** when sharing a room with two adults.
- A valid birth certificate or passport is required to confirm the child's/infant's age at booking.
- **Unaccompanied minors** (under 18) are not permitted on packages unless accompanied by a guardian.
- Child and infant discounts do not apply to flights, which are priced per the airline's own policy.
