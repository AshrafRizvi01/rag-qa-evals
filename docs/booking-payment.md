# Booking & Payment Policy

- A minimum deposit of **20% of the total package value** is required to confirm a booking.
- The remaining balance must be paid in full at least **15 days before** the travel date.
- Accepted payment methods: credit/debit card, net banking, and UPI.
- Bookings made within **15 days** of the travel date must be paid in full at the time of booking.
- Prices are confirmed only after full payment is received; partial payments do not lock in pricing.
