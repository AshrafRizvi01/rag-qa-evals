# Force Majeure Policy

- Sunshine Travels is not liable for trip disruptions caused by events beyond its control, including **natural disasters, political unrest, pandemics, and government travel restrictions**.
- Affected travelers will be offered a **rescheduled travel date** or a **credit voucher** valid for 12 months, where possible.
- Cash refunds during force majeure events are subject to recovery of costs already paid to third-party vendors (hotels, airlines, etc.).
- Sunshine Travels will communicate updates and available options as soon as practical once a force majeure event is confirmed.
