# Luggage & Baggage Policy

- Standard baggage allowance is **1 check-in bag (up to 23kg)** and **1 cabin bag (up to 7kg)** per traveler, unless the airline specifies otherwise.
- Excess baggage is charged directly by the airline or hotel at their prevailing rates.
- **Prohibited items** (e.g., flammables, weapons, hazardous materials) must not be packed, per airline and destination country regulations.
- Sunshine Travels is not liable for **lost, delayed, or damaged baggage**; such claims must be filed directly with the airline or hotel.
- Valuables and travel documents should be carried in cabin baggage at all times.
